Frequently asked questions

What services do you provide?

Profile assessment, country selection, university guidance, admission support and visa assistance.

Do you provide free counseling?

Yes. Use the form below to book a free consultation.

Which countries do you support?

Explore 25 country snapshots above. A counselor can help narrow the list by course, budget, language and intake.

When should I start planning?

Start as early as you can. Deadlines, document requirements and intake dates differ by university and country, so confirm them for each program.

Do I need an English-language test?

It depends on your chosen institution and program. Check the course's official entry requirements before booking a test.

Can you guarantee admission, scholarships or a visa?

No. Decisions are made by universities and immigration authorities. We can help you understand requirements and prepare a strong, complete application.

How do I compare the total cost?

Consider tuition, housing, food, insurance, travel and other living expenses. Use the destination comparison as a starting point and verify current figures with official sources.

How will I know you received my consultation request?

Requests will be submitted through the Google Form linked below. The team can receive email alerts after response notifications are enabled in Google Forms.
